## M4 — Mission recorder
Persist event streams, derive statistics, timelines and eventually spatial analysis/heat maps.

Exit (agreed 2026-10-13): with recording enabled, one real HITMAN mission attempt on the observed build is recorded by the Relay node into a local recording that is successfully finalized and survives the termination of the game and of the BEAM node; a fresh node without the game replays that recording into Lifecycle state equal to, and a mission report byte-identical to, the live node's state and report captured at quiescence; and the replay presents a transparent completeness account that separates what the recorder wrote from what it knows it missed, what is unverifiable, and what native-side evidence loss remains unknown — so that no recording is presented as complete beyond what the evidence shows.

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE (2026-10-13).** The goal line and the exit criterion above are recorded as originally written. How the stages satisfy the milestone (`docs/design/M4_MISSION_RECORDER.md`, ADR 0008): **M4-A** (section 17) — evidence recording with explicit transport metadata: a recording-grade `MissionSession` subscription with session-assigned record and connection numbers and the evidence's own receipt time, and `GlacierRelay.Recording.Recorder` behind `GLACIER_RELAY_RECORD=1` writing format-1 segmented NDJSON with header, explicit gap and recovery lines, a footer only through the close protocol, segment hashes and a `CLOSED` marker. **M4-B** (section 18) — deterministic replay through the shared pure `Session.Fold`: the verifying reader with conservative record precedence, the replay with re-validation, and an explicit completeness account that keeps recorder-level, stream-level and native-side loss apart. **M4-B hardening** (section 19) — trusted durable boundaries on every continuation header (cross-checked before use) and a typed close-reason vocabulary (exact only within it, qualified evidence otherwise). **The live M4 recording** (sections 20–21) — one HITMAN Paris attempt on the observed build durably recorded, finalized, reconciled and replayed in a fresh node. **M4-C** (section 22) — evidence-derived statistics and cross-recording comparisons that inherit the evidence class. The actual evidence: 12 durable session records from the live experiment; nine matching native, BEAM and recorder envelopes with zero field mismatches and no unexpected gaps; term-equal replayed Lifecycle state and summary/report data; byte-identical legacy and B7 report text; a recorder-authoritative completeness account for the observed recording scope (native drops unknown to the recording, reconciled to zero externally); M0 restoration verified 26/26; 437 Elixir tests in five consecutive successful runs for M4-C; deterministic text and JSON analytics across fresh VMs. Code: glacier-relay `main` — M4-A `1b109da` on `9653128`, M4-B `48dcf16` on `72c63a0`, hardening `9b7dac8` on `7a858e4`, M4-C `6fc3662`; the native adapter is **unchanged at the M2 checkpoint** (ZHMModSDK `relay/m2` `c5152848`, DLL `6047a9a3278fdd725c55bdc16ac47f832436afb2f5a6f2260488a436a6009d65`). **Scope of the success: one real recording and replay experiment on the documented engine build, one location and one route, and recordings in the kilobyte range; it claims nothing about other game builds, routes, loading conditions or recording volumes.** Live segment rollover, recorder restart, write failure and budget exhaustion were exercised offline only; mailbox bounds are soft; native drops are unknown to a recording unless reconciled; some close reasons are qualified; actor identities are not unique; completion, death, rating, attribution, inventory and current state stay outside the vocabulary; spatial data is not available and heat maps are not implemented — M4-D spatial research remains an independently authorized future investigation (section 23.5). ## M6 — Second Hitman
Reproduce and stabilize the upstream experimental `Hitmen` second-player entity on the current game.

Exit: one game instance contains a controllable local player and a distinct externally driven Hitman representation.

## M7 — Network ghost
Two PCs, same mission. Each instance renders the other player's externally driven Hitman.

Exit: bidirectional movement/input representation survives a bounded test session.

## M8 — Shared-world slice
Synchronize a deliberately small set of semantic facts: selected doors, one NPC lifecycle, one item lifecycle, and objective state.

Exit: both clients converge after scripted interactions.

## M9 — Constrained co-op
Complete one selected mission under documented restrictions.

Exit criteria must include desync instrumentation and a repeatable test scenario.

## Beyond M9
Only after measurement: broader actor authority, inventories, combat/projectiles, ragdolls, disguise/enforcer semantics, mission scripting, reconnect/snapshots, Freelancer research, matchmaking, plugins, public hosting.

No date commitments are attached to research milestones.
